“FUCK!” Cat snapped its paw away from the pan handle and ran to the sink.
“Fuck that hurts…” it muttered, submerging its paw in lukewarm water. Looking down, some of its fur had come apart, sticking to the hot pan handle.

The apples.
Compulsively, it checked the fridge.
Twelve. It counted.
It exhaled and shut the door.

It stared at its reflection in the door of the fridge, brows furrowed. Its paw throbbed and felt as if someone had sharply pinched its skin. Its usual dark grey fur was singed black, its pawpads swelled up and beginning to blister.
